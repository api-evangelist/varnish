Security and observability are core parts of Varnish Enterprise. Starting with release 6.0.18r5, vmod-ratelimit , vmod-cwt , varnish-otel , vmod-kv and vmod-nodes are included in every Varnish Enterprise license. We are adding these capabilities to your standard toolkit to provide more value out of the box.
