Modern CI/CD has outgrown the network. Compute has become elastic, but data hasn’t. The software dependencies produced during development, testing, building, and packaging—collectively referred to as “artifacts,” including modules, containers, Helm charts, and other related scripts and binaries—are growing faster than the bandwidth available to move them.
