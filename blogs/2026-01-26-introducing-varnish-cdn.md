<p>For too long, European organisations have been forced into a compromise: accept the performance of global CDNs while inheriting their legal and sovereignty risks. Most global providers operate as an opaque ‘black box’; you have limited visibility into where traffic is routed or who has access to logs. Between the US CLOUD Act and the tightening requirements of NIS2, this lack of control has turned a technical headache into a significant compliance liability.</p>
