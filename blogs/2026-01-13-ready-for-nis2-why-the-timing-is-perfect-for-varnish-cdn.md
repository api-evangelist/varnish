<p style="text-align: left;">The need for digital sovereignty in Europe has never been greater. Discussions surrounding <a href="https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive">NIS2</a>, the US CLOUD Act, and supply chain security have made one thing clear: There is a gap in the market. Europe needs an independent CDN that combines technical excellence with legal certainty.</p> 
<p>Some time ago, we decided to fill that gap. Today, we want to give you an update on where we stand.</p>
