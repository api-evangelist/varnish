<p>GitHub is under more pressure than ever, and the issue isn’t just growth. It’s the way modern development systems generate traffic. GitHub has acknowledged that recent instability occurred during periods of “extremely rapid usage growth,” driven by load spikes, architectural coupling, and difficulty shedding excess traffic (see <a href="https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/addressing-githubs-recent-availability-issues-2/">GitHub’s own post</a> on recent availability issues).&nbsp;</p>
